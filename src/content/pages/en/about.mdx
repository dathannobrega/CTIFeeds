I'm Dathan Nobrega. I write about threat research, detection engineering and incident
response, and publish each analysis together with the artifacts other analysts can use:
machine-readable IoCs, ATT&CK mappings and detection rules.

## What you will find here

- **Research and campaigns:** analyses of activity observed on my own infrastructure
  (honeypots, labs) or in public sources, each campaign with CSV and STIX 2.1 downloads.
- **Walkthroughs:** step-by-step labs, from a Sigma rule to an alert, or enriching alerts
  with public threat intel feeds.
- **Notes:** shorter technical write-ups.

## Ground rules

Everything published here is personal research built on infrastructure and data that are mine
or public. Nothing comes from any employer's or client's environment: no telemetry, no
incidents, no internal architecture. Indicators are shared as **TLP:CLEAR** only, defanged in
the text and validated automatically before publication.

Opinions are my own.
